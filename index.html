Recognition Review — Know These When You See Them

You do not need to write these from memory for this quiz, but you should be able to recognize them and know what they mean.

Sentence-Final Particles: 嗎 / 吧 / 呢 / 噢

Focus on the feeling and function of each particle, not just a one-word English translation.

Particle Main use Example What it sounds like
嗎 / 吗
ma		 Turns a statement into a yes/no question. 你認識他嗎？
Do you know him?		 Neutral yes/no question.
吧
ba		 Makes a suggestion, softens a command, or shows uncertainty. 我們走吧。
Let's go.

他是學生吧？
He's a student, right?		 “Let’s…,” “how about…,” or “probably / right?”
呢
ne		 Asks “what about…?” or keeps attention on an ongoing situation/topic. 我很好，你呢？
I’m good. How about you?		 “What about…?” / keeps the topic open.
噢
ō / ò		 Shows realization, understanding, or a reaction. It is not a regular question marker. 噢，我知道了。
Oh, I see / I got it.		 “Oh!” — realization or response.
