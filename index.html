Part 3 — Small Words You Need to Use

These are very common words from Lessons 1–2. Focus on the basic use in simple sentences.

也 yě
also / too
我也是學生。
I am also a student.
都 dōu
all / both
我們都是學生。
We are all students.
給 / 给 gěi
to give; for/to someone
我給媽媽打電話。
I call my mom.
不 bù
not — usually for present/future or habits
我不住在紐約。
I do not live in New York.
只 zhǐ
only
我只有一個哥哥。
I only have one older brother.
可是 kěshì
but
我很忙，可是我很高興。
I am busy, but I am happy.

Sentence-final particles

嗎 / 吗 ma yes/no question
你認識他嗎？ Do you know him?
吧 ba suggestion / softer tone
我們走吧。 Let’s go.
呢 ne what about…?
我很好，你呢？ I’m good. How about you?
噢 ō / ò Oh! / I see.
噢，我知道了。 Oh, I see.
